2. Semantic model

The model uses a star-schema approach. FactTickets is the central fact table, supported by dimensions for date, agent, category and priority.

FactTicketsTicket-level measures and fields such as Ticket_ID, Created_Date, Resolved_At, Priority, Agent_ID, Category_ID, SLA_Met, Resolution_Hours, CSAT and Repeat_Flag.
DimDateDate, month, week, day and other calendar attributes used for time-based reporting.
DimAgentAgent ID, agent name, team and related support attributes.
DimCategory / DimPriorityReusable category and priority dimensions for filtering and grouping operational KPIs.

3. Relationship design

  • DimDate[Date] → FactTickets[Created_Date]
  • DimAgent[Agent_ID] → FactTickets[Agent_ID]
  • DimCategory[Category_ID] → FactTickets[Category_ID]
  • DimPriority[Priority] → FactTickets[Priority]

Relationships are designed as one-to-many, single-direction filters from dimensions into the fact table.

4. Core BI measures

MeasureBusiness purpose
TicketsTotal ticket volume in the selected filter context.
Resolved TicketsNumber of tickets with a valid resolution timestamp.
SLA Adherence %Share of applicable tickets resolved within SLA.
SLA BreachesCount of tickets that exceeded the applicable SLA target.
Average Resolution HoursAverage elapsed resolution time for resolved tickets.
Average CSATAverage customer satisfaction score across available responses.
Repeat Ticket Rate %Share of tickets flagged as repeat cases.
P1 SLA Breach %Focused exception KPI for the highest-priority tickets.

5. Recommended report pages

Executive OverviewTicket volume, SLA adherence, average resolution time, CSAT and repeat-ticket rate with date and priority slicers.
SLA & OperationsSLA performance by priority, team and month, with breach-focused exception analysis.
Demand & WorkloadTicket volume by category, hour, day and month to identify workload patterns.
Team PerformanceAgent and team-level resolution, SLA and CSAT views with appropriate volume thresholds.

6. Data-quality controls

  • Duplicate Ticket_ID detection before reporting.
  • Created and resolved timestamp validation.
  • Priority and SLA-value validation.
  • Missing-value review for category and agent fields.
  • Curated row-count reconciliation against the ETL output.

7. Business question → BI output

Business questionBI output
Are tickets being resolved within SLA?SLA Adherence %, SLA Breaches and trend visuals.
Which priorities need investigation?Priority-level SLA breach and resolution analysis.
Is performance changing over time?Monthly ticket, SLA and CSAT trends.
Where is workload concentrated?Category, hour and day workload views.
How is the team performing?Agent/team performance with volume context.

8. Performance considerations

  • Keep the model at a clear ticket-level grain.
  • Prefer reusable measures over repeated calculated logic in visuals.
  • Use dimensions for filtering rather than duplicating descriptive fields unnecessarily.
  • Keep transformations in the ETL/curation layer where practical.
  • Validate filter context and measure results against SQL checks before publishing.
Project scope: This page documents the BI model and reporting design for a portfolio demonstration. It does not claim an enterprise Power BI deployment, production data warehouse, OLAP cube, or live scheduled refresh infrastructure.
