Semantic model

The reporting model follows a star-schema approach. FactTickets acts as the central ticket-level table, supported by dimensions for dates, agents, categories, priorities, channels, customer segments and regions.

FactTickets

Ticket-level fields such as Ticket_ID, Created_Date, Resolved_At, Priority, Agent_ID, Category_ID, SLA_Met, Resolution_Hours, CSAT and Repeat_Flag.

DimDate

Date, month, week, day and other calendar attributes used for time-based reporting.

DimAgent

Agent ID, agent name, team and related support attributes used for team-level analysis.

DimCategory / DimPriority

Reusable category and priority dimensions for filtering and grouping operational KPIs.

DimChannel / DimSegment / DimRegion

Additional dimensions supporting channel, customer segment and geographic analysis.

Relationship design

  • DimDate[Date] → FactTickets[Created_Date]
  • DimAgent[Agent_ID] → FactTickets[Agent_ID]
  • DimCategory[Category_ID] → FactTickets[Category_ID]
  • DimPriority[Priority] → FactTickets[Priority]
  • DimChannel[Channel] → FactTickets[Channel]
  • DimSegment[Segment] → FactTickets[Customer_Segment]
  • DimRegion[Region] → FactTickets[Region]

Relationships are designed as one-to-many, single-direction filters from dimensions into the ticket-level fact table.

Core BI measures

Measure Business purpose
Tickets Total ticket volume within the selected filter context.
Resolved Tickets Number of tickets with a valid resolution timestamp.
SLA Adherence % Share of applicable tickets resolved within the defined SLA target.
SLA Breaches Number of tickets that exceeded the applicable SLA target.
Average Resolution Hours Average elapsed resolution time for resolved tickets.
Average CSAT Average customer satisfaction score across available responses.
Repeat Ticket Rate % Share of tickets identified as repeat cases.
P1 SLA Breach % Focused exception KPI for the highest-priority tickets.
Average First Response Minutes Measures initial response time before ticket resolution.
Escalation Rate % Shows the share of tickets escalated during handling.

Recommended report pages

Executive Overview

Ticket volume, SLA adherence, average resolution time, CSAT and repeat-ticket rate with date and priority filters.

SLA & Operations

SLA performance by priority, team and month with breach-focused exception analysis.

Demand & Workload

Ticket volume by category, channel, customer segment, hour, day and month to identify workload patterns.

Team Performance

Agent and team-level resolution, SLA and CSAT views with appropriate ticket-volume context.

Data-quality controls

  • Duplicate Ticket_ID detection before reporting.
  • Created and resolved timestamp validation.
  • Priority and SLA-value validation.
  • Missing-value review for category and agent fields.
  • Curated row-count reconciliation against the ETL output.

Business question → BI output

Business question BI output
Are tickets being resolved within SLA? SLA Adherence %, SLA Breaches and trend visuals.
Which priorities need investigation? Priority-level SLA breach and resolution analysis.
Is performance changing over time? Monthly ticket, SLA and CSAT trends.
Where is workload concentrated? Category, hour and day workload views.
How is the team performing? Agent and team performance with ticket-volume context.

Performance considerations

  • Keep the model at a clear ticket-level grain.
  • Prefer reusable measures over repeated calculation logic in individual visuals.
  • Use dimensions for filtering rather than unnecessarily duplicating descriptive fields.
  • Keep transformations in the ETL and data-preparation layer where practical.
  • Validate filter context and measure results against SQL checks before reporting.
Project scope: This page documents the Power BI model and reporting design used in the portfolio project. It does not claim an enterprise Power BI deployment, production data warehouse, OLAP platform or live scheduled refresh infrastructure.
