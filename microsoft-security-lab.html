Investigation objective

The objective was to build a repeatable workflow for moving from a security alert to an investigation result: understand alert context, prioritize severity, inspect associated evidence, query relevant telemetry, identify the likely technique, record the response action and measure resolution performance.

Investigation workflow

The analysis follows a simple incident-response sequence rather than treating the dashboard as the end product.

01

Alert intake

Review alert name, severity, source, affected device/entity and available evidence before deciding investigation priority.

02

Evidence review

Correlate alert context with related entities and activity to understand what happened and whether additional investigation is required.

03

KQL analysis

Use filtering, projection, aggregation and entity-level queries to isolate relevant security activity and identify patterns.

04

Outcome & KPI

Record investigation status and response action, then analyze severity, resolution rate and MTTR for operational reporting.

KQL investigation

Queries were used to filter, summarize and prioritize security events before interpreting the results.

Severity prioritization

SecurityAlert
| where TimeGenerated > ago(7d)
| where AlertSeverity == "High"
| project TimeGenerated, AlertSeverity, AlertName, ProviderName
| order by TimeGenerated desc

Alert distribution

SecurityAlert
| where TimeGenerated > ago(7d)
| summarize AlertCount = count() by AlertSeverity
| order by AlertCount desc

Entity-level investigation

SecurityAlert
| where TimeGenerated > ago(7d)
| summarize AlertCount = count() by CompromisedEntity
| order by AlertCount desc

Key findings

Severity required prioritization.

Half of the investigated alerts were high severity, making severity-based triage an important first filter for investigation workload.

Endpoint activity dominated investigation.

Malware, PowerShell, credential-access and network scenarios required endpoint-focused evidence review.

Investigation time became a KPI.

MTTR was tracked alongside alert severity and resolution status to connect technical investigation with operational performance.

Structured evidence improves repeatability.

Capturing category, detection source, technique, status and response action makes later trend analysis easier.
