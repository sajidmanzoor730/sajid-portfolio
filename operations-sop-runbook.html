Operations SOP & Runbook

A structured operational playbook covering onboarding, access reviews, recurring checks, escalation and handoffs with clear ownership and evidence requirements.

Project scope: This page demonstrates SOP and runbook design principles. It does not contain real employee credentials, customer information, internal company procedures or confidential operational records.

Core operational procedures

01 · Onboarding

Confirm the request owner, required access, documentation and onboarding checklist before the process begins.

Owner Access Checklist

02 · Access Review

Verify required systems and remove unnecessary access through the appropriate approval and request process.

Security Audit Approval

03 · Recurring Checks

Review queues, overdue tasks, failed processes, data-quality exceptions and SLA-related issues on a defined schedule.

Daily SLA Quality

04 · Escalation

Capture impact, evidence, owner, next action and escalation timing before handing an issue to the appropriate team.

Incident Evidence Handoff

Standard runbook structure

Step Required action Owner Evidence
1 Receive and review the operational request. Assigned owner Request or ticket reference
2 Confirm scope, priority and required approvals. Process owner Approval or request details
3 Complete the required operational checks. Assigned operator Checklist or system record
4 Document the outcome and any exceptions. Assigned operator Notes or supporting evidence
5 Escalate unresolved issues when defined thresholds are reached. Process owner Escalation record
6 Complete the handoff and confirm next ownership. Outgoing owner Handoff note

Daily operations checklist

  • Review open and overdue operational work.
  • Check SLA exceptions and aging items.
  • Review failed or incomplete automated processes.
  • Check for data-quality issues requiring correction.
  • Confirm ownership for unresolved items.
  • Document important exceptions and follow-up actions.

Escalation requirements

Impact

Clearly describe what is affected and the operational consequence.

Evidence

Include relevant records, timestamps, screenshots or system references where appropriate.

Owner

Identify the current owner and the team responsible for the next action.

Next action

State what needs to happen next and whether a deadline or SLA applies.

Handoff checklist

  • Current status is documented.
  • Outstanding actions are clearly listed.
  • Relevant evidence is attached or referenced.
  • Next owner is identified.
  • Priority and SLA expectations are clear.
  • Follow-up date is recorded when required.
