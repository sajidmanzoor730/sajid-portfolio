KPI Framework

Definitions used for the dashboard calculations.

Completion Rate

Completed and closed records divided by total records.

SLA Achievement

Records where actual resolution time is within the applicable SLA target.

QA Accuracy

Assessed records achieving the dashboard QA threshold.

Rework Rate

Records flagged for additional work based on quality outcomes.

Data Validity

Records passing the available data-quality validation checks.

Average Resolution

Average recorded resolution time across the current dataset view.
