KPI Operations Dashboard

A practical operations analytics project focused on KPI reporting, operational performance, SLA tracking, quality monitoring, and structured decision support.

← All Projects

Project overview

This project demonstrates how operational data can be organized into a structured KPI reporting workflow. The focus is not simply on displaying numbers, but on defining meaningful metrics, validating the underlying data, and presenting information in a format that supports operational reviews.

Business purpose

Operations teams often work with large amounts of activity, quality, status, and SLA information. Without a consistent reporting structure, it can be difficult to understand whether work is progressing as expected or where operational attention is required.

The purpose of this dashboard is to provide a structured view of operational KPIs and create a repeatable approach for monitoring performance.

Business questions

The dashboard is designed around practical questions that can be asked during an operational or KPI review.

Completion: How much of the assigned operational work has been completed?
Quality: Is completed work meeting the required quality standard?
SLA: Is work being completed within the expected service level?
Status: What work remains open, pending, completed, or requires attention?
Trend: Is operational performance improving, declining, or remaining stable?
Workload: Where is operational activity concentrated?

KPI framework

The dashboard uses a KPI framework that separates activity, completion, quality, service level, and status indicators. Actual values can be added once the underlying real dataset is finalized.

Completion Rate

Measures the proportion of assigned or received work that has been completed during the reporting period.

Purpose: Monitor throughput and completion performance.

QA Accuracy

Measures the quality of completed work against the applicable review or quality criteria.

Purpose: Monitor quality and identify areas requiring review.

SLA Achievement

Measures whether operational work is completed within the defined service-level target.

Purpose: Monitor timeliness and identify SLA pressure.

Operational Status

Tracks the distribution of work across relevant operational states such as open, pending, completed, rejected, or escalated.

Purpose: Understand workload position and operational backlog.

Data quality & validation

Reliable KPI reporting starts with reliable source data. The project therefore treats data quality as part of the analytical workflow rather than an afterthought.

Validation checks

Duplicate detection Identify repeated records that could distort operational counts or KPI calculations.
Missing-value checks Review important fields for blanks or incomplete records before reporting.
Status validation Confirm that operational status values follow the expected reporting definitions.
Date validation Check date and timestamp fields before using them for SLA or trend analysis.
KPI validation Confirm that calculated KPIs use the correct population, denominator, and business rules.
Reconciliation Compare dashboard totals against the prepared analytical dataset to identify inconsistencies.

Dashboard design

Designed for operational review

The dashboard structure prioritizes the information an operations or management team would typically need during a KPI review.

  • High-level KPI visibility for quick performance review.
  • Completion and workload monitoring.
  • Quality and accuracy monitoring.
  • SLA performance and potential service-level pressure.
  • Operational status and outstanding work.
  • Trend analysis for identifying changes over time.
  • Structured reporting that can support further investigation.

Analytical dimensions

KPIs become more useful when they can be analyzed across relevant operational dimensions rather than viewed only as overall totals.

Time

Analyze performance by reporting period to identify trends, changes, and recurring operational patterns.

Status

Compare completed, open, pending, escalated, or other operational states.

Quality

Examine quality or accuracy results alongside workload and completion activity.

SLA

Compare service-level performance and identify where timeliness requires additional attention.

Reporting structure

From operational records to KPIs

The reporting structure separates the underlying operational records from the calculated KPI layer. This makes the analysis easier to validate and allows the same source data to support multiple reporting views.

The intended structure is:

01

Source records

Raw operational activity and status information.

02

Clean dataset

Validated and standardized analytical records.

03

KPI layer

Defined calculations and business rules.

04

Dashboard

Business-readable reporting and analysis.

Analyst approach

The project follows a practical analyst mindset: first understand what the data represents, then validate it, define the KPIs, analyze the results, and only then present the information in dashboard form.

  • Understand the operational process and reporting requirements.
  • Identify the fields required for each KPI.
  • Validate source data before calculating metrics.
  • Define KPI formulas consistently.
  • Compare performance across meaningful dimensions.
  • Investigate unusual or unexpected patterns.
  • Present findings in a format that is easy for non-technical stakeholders to understand.

Business insight framework

The dashboard is intended to help move operational reporting from simple activity counts toward questions that support decision-making.

  • Where is operational workload increasing?
  • Which areas require additional quality attention?
  • Where are SLA targets under pressure?
  • Are completion and quality moving together?
  • Which operational statuses are creating backlog?
  • Are performance changes temporary or part of a longer trend?
Data scope: This portfolio page intentionally does not display fabricated performance figures. Real KPI values, charts, and findings will be added after the underlying dataset and calculations are finalized.
