Public IT Helpdesk Dataset

Explore the official aggregated MCG HelpIT Tickets dataset published by Montgomery County Government. This page links to the source rather than displaying the old generated ticket-level demo file.

Dataset notes
  • Published by Montgomery County Government, Maryland.
  • Contains summary rows grouped by fiscal year, report section and item.
  • Includes published counts and percentages where available.
  • Does not expose individual ticket IDs, ticket-level timestamps, agent records, SLA targets or individual CSAT scores.
  • The old generated ticket-level CSV is a legacy demonstration artifact and is not the live dashboard source.

What you can analyze

  • Published request-type counts, such as Email Support and VPN.
  • Contact counts by source.
  • Published contact counts by department.
  • Survey summary rows as reported by the source.
  • Differences across fiscal years, where comparable rows are available.

Important: do not combine totals from different report sections because the sections may overlap. Survey summary counts are not the same as individual CSAT ratings.

Data provenance

Source: MCG HelpIT Tickets — Montgomery County Open Data.

The live dashboard fetches the official source at runtime. If the source is unavailable, the dashboard reports an error instead of silently displaying generated data.
