Detailed case studies

How I approach an analytics problem.

The strongest projects show the reasoning behind the output: what needed to be understood, how the data was handled and what the result was used for.

CASE 01 · HELPDESK

From ticket export to KPI dashboard

  • Profiled and cleaned 3,672 raw rows.
  • Resolved duplicates to 3,600 unique tickets.
  • Calculated SLA, CSAT, repeat-ticket and MTTR metrics.
  • Presented findings through SQL, Python and dashboard views.
Outcome: a repeatable KPI workflow that connects data quality with operational reporting.
CASE 02 · BI VALIDATION

Validate the number before reporting it

  • Profiled source and cleaned datasets.
  • Checked issue types and row-level findings.
  • Used SQL/Python reconciliation logic.
  • Documented data-quality gates for BI reporting.
Outcome: a validation layer designed to make KPI reporting more trustworthy.
CASE 03 · SECURITY ANALYTICS

Turn alerts into measurable investigation work

  • Investigated 12 security alerts.
  • Prioritized 6 high-severity records.
  • Used KQL for filtering, correlation and aggregation.
  • Mapped investigation scenarios to MITRE ATT&CK techniques.
Outcome: 10 resolved investigations and 25.3-minute average MTTR across resolved records.
