How I troubleshoot

Four steps I follow on most cases, in this order.

  1. ReproduceUnderstand the reportCollect the symptom, who is affected, when it started and what has changed.
  2. IsolateFind the layerWork out whether the cause is client-side code, an API, a database query or the network.
  3. ConfirmCheck the evidenceUse logs, Postman requests, SQL queries and Wireshark captures to confirm the cause.
  4. Hand offFix or escalate clearlyResolve it, or escalate with the narrowed-down cause, steps to reproduce and impact, then document the fix.

Worked example: one-way audio

Illustrative walkthrough, not a specific customer case
Symptom
One party can hear the other, but the other party cannot hear them.
What I check
Whether SIP signaling completes (INVITE, 180, 200 OK, ACK), then whether RTP appears in both directions in a Wireshark capture.
Likely cause
RTP in one direction is blocked or misrouted, often by firewall or NAT handling of the media ports or the address in the SDP.
Resolution
Correct the media routing or firewall/NAT rule, then place a test call and confirm audio flows both ways.
