How I troubleshoot

Four steps I follow on most cases, in this order.

  1. ReproduceUnderstand the reportCollect the symptom, who is affected, when it started and what has changed.
  2. IsolateFind the layerWork out whether the cause is client-side code, an API, a database query or the network.
  3. ConfirmCheck the evidenceUse logs, Postman requests, SQL queries and Wireshark captures to confirm the cause.
  4. Hand offFix or escalate clearlyResolve it, or escalate with the narrowed-down cause, steps to reproduce and impact, then document the fix.
