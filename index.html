Working approach

Four steps I follow on every project, in this order.

  1. InspectUnderstand the source dataReview fields, missing values, duplicates, dates, definitions and assumptions before calculating metrics.
  2. TransformBuild a repeatable data processUse Python, Pandas and SQL to clean, validate, transform, reconcile and analyze the data.
  3. ValidateCheck the numbersReconcile KPIs across the source data, analytical layer and dashboard before using them for reporting.
  4. CommunicatePresent the result clearlyTurn validated metrics into focused dashboards and concise findings that answer business questions.
