Executive Operations Dashboard

A management view of the open workload: what is overdue, how old it is, who owns it and which items are waiting on a decision.

Data note: This dashboard runs on a synthetic Executive Operations Dataset of 6,000 records covering January–September 2026. The raw file (6,060 rows) was de-duplicated on Record_ID to produce the clean file used here. It contains no confidential employer or customer data; findings illustrate the analysis method, not real business results.

Key findings

Calculated on the full dataset, independent of the filters below. The data is synthetic, so these show the analysis approach, not real business results.

Finding 1

The backlog is old, and mostly late

509 records are still active; 422 of them (82.9%) are past their due date. 193 (37.9%) have been open for more than 90 days, and the oldest is 272 days old.

Action: Clear the records older than 90 days first, before taking on new work.

Finding 2

Overdue rate roughly doubled after June

The monthly overdue rate stayed between 7.0% and 8.5% from January to June, then rose to 11.9% in July, 16.6% in August and 14.2% in September.

Action: Find out what changed in July (capacity, intake volume or process) and track the rate monthly.

Finding 3

Complexity predicts SLA breaches

High-complexity records breach SLA 44.7% of the time against 11.5% for low complexity, about 3.9 times as often.

Action: Set due dates and staffing for high-complexity work separately instead of using one standard target.

Finding 4

Back Office is the weakest team

Back Office breaches SLA 31.1% of the time against 21.9% for Operations, and also has the highest overdue rate (11.4%, against 8.5% for Customer Success).

Action: Review Back Office capacity and process first.

Finding 5

Priority is not protecting urgent work

49 of the 52 active P1 records (94.2%) are overdue, a higher share than active P4 records (74.5%).

Action: Add a daily P1 review and an escalation path so urgent items are not left to age.

How the KPIs are defined

Definitions used by the dashboard, plus SQL that reproduces the headline figures.

Active workload
Records with Status Open, In Progress or Pending (SLA_Status = Open).
Active & overdue
Active records with Overdue_Flag = Yes.
Average age (active)
Average Aging_Days across active records only.
Overdue rate
Records with Overdue_Flag = Yes ÷ records (includes records completed late).
SLA breach rate
Breached ÷ (Met + Breached). Records still open are excluded.
Follow-up rate
Records with Follow_Up = Yes ÷ records.
SQL to reproduce these numbers (SQLite syntax)
SELECT
  SUM(CASE WHEN Status IN ('Open','In Progress','Pending') THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS active,
  SUM(CASE WHEN Status IN ('Open','In Progress','Pending') AND Overdue_Flag = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS active_overdue,
  ROUND(AVG(CASE WHEN Status IN ('Open','In Progress','Pending') THEN Aging_Days END), 1) AS avg_age_active_days,
  ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN Overdue_Flag = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(*), 1) AS overdue_pct,
  ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN SLA_Status = 'Breached' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
        / SUM(CASE WHEN SLA_Status IN ('Met','Breached') THEN 1 ELSE 0 END), 1) AS sla_breach_pct,
  ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN Follow_Up = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(*), 1) AS follow_up_pct
FROM executive_operations;
SELECT strftime('%Y-%m', Created_Date) AS month,
  ROUND(100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN Overdue_Flag = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(*), 1) AS overdue_pct
FROM executive_operations
GROUP BY month
ORDER BY month;

SQL behind the findings

The backlog and overdue findings above come from these queries. The results below were produced by running these queries on the clean CSVs (SQLite). Reproduce them with python3 sql/run_sql.py.

exec active backlog

-- Finding: the backlog is old, and mostly late
SELECT COUNT(*)                                             AS active_records,
       SUM(Overdue_Flag = 'Yes')                            AS overdue,
       ROUND(100.0 * SUM(Overdue_Flag = 'Yes') / COUNT(*), 1) AS overdue_pct,
       SUM(Aging_Days > 90)                                 AS open_over_90_days,
       MAX(Aging_Days)                                      AS oldest_days
FROM exec_ops
WHERE Status NOT IN ('Completed', 'Closed');
