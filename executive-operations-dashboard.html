Operations Analytics

Executive Operations Dashboard

A structured reporting framework for giving operations teams a clear view of workload, priorities, ownership, deadlines, follow-ups and performance indicators.

Project Overview

This project focuses on the analytical layer behind an executive operations dashboard. The goal is to turn day-to-day operational records into a concise reporting view that supports prioritisation, accountability and management review.

Business Purpose Consolidate operational activity into a structured reporting view that makes priorities, ownership and outstanding work easier to monitor.
Analyst Focus Data preparation, KPI definition, workload analysis, status monitoring, exception identification and management reporting.
Reporting Outcome A repeatable dashboard structure that connects operational records with measurable performance indicators and follow-up actions.

Business Questions

The dashboard is designed around questions that an operations or business team would regularly need to answer.

  • What work is currently open or in progress?
  • Which priorities require immediate attention?
  • Which activities are approaching their due dates?
  • Which owners or teams have outstanding actions?
  • Where are recurring delays or follow-up requirements appearing?
  • How is operational workload changing over time?
  • Which KPIs should be reviewed in weekly management meetings?
  • Which exceptions should be escalated for further investigation?

KPI Framework

Core measures that can be calculated from the underlying operational records.

Workload Total records, activities or items handled during the selected reporting period.
Completion Rate Share of operational records completed within the reporting period.
Aging Time elapsed between record creation and current status or completion.
SLA Achievement Share of applicable records completed within the defined service target.
Open Actions Outstanding work requiring completion, follow-up or escalation.
Follow-up Rate Share of records requiring additional action after the initial activity.

Analytical Dimensions

Dimensions used to segment operational performance and identify meaningful differences between groups.

Team Owner Priority Status Category Region Channel Date Week Month

Analyst Workflow

The reporting process follows a repeatable analytics workflow.

01
Collect Bring together operational records from the relevant source systems or structured exports.
02
Validate Check duplicates, missing fields, dates, status values and other data-quality conditions.
03
Analyse Calculate KPIs, segment workload and identify trends, exceptions and recurring patterns.
04
Report Present the results through dashboards and concise management reporting.
