Candidate Sourcing & Pipeline Analytics

A structured analytics framework for technical staffing workflows, connecting client requirements, candidate stages, aging, ownership, follow-ups, SLA/TAT and fulfillment reporting.

Why this project: My staffing operations experience involved active client requirements, candidate records, weekly staffing requests and pipeline tracking. This project translates that workflow into an analyst-friendly reporting model without presenting simulated records as workplace results.

Core analytical views

Requirement pipeline

Track open roles by client, priority, owner, status and age so active demand is visible in one place.

Candidate funnel

Follow candidates through sourced, screened, submitted, interviewed, selected and closed stages.

SLA & TAT

Compare request dates, target dates and completion dates to identify delayed requirements and recurring bottlenecks.

Fulfillment analysis

Connect candidate movement to requirement outcomes and identify where the pipeline loses momentum.

Data model

  • Requirement: requirement ID, client, role, priority, owner, opened date, target date and status.
  • Candidate: candidate ID, source, stage, submission date, interview status and outcome.
  • Activity: follow-up date, action, owner, activity type and timestamp.
  • Metrics: aging, SLA/TAT, stage conversion, fulfillment and workload.

Workplace relevance

This project reflects the same analytical questions that matter in staffing operations: What is open? Where is each requirement in the pipeline? Which items are aging? Who owns the next action? Where are SLA or fulfillment delays appearing?

It demonstrates how operational staffing data can be structured for repeatable reporting and decision support rather than treated as a simple candidate list.
