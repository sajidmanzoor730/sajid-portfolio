# Dashboard design (to be built once real results exist)

Audience: a deputy commissioner or operations manager who has two minutes.

| Page | Visuals | Question | Decision it supports |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Overview | KPI cards: requests, % closed, % closed within 7 days, median hours to close. Daily volume with 7-day average | Is service on track this month? | Whether anything needs escalating today |
| 2. What and where | Top 15 complaint types; borough comparison; top 3 per borough | What drives demand, and does it differ by borough? | Where to direct inspection and outreach resources |
| 3. Agency performance | Closure rate, % closed within 24h and 7 days, median hours, backlog older than 14 days, by agency; repeat-location list | Who is slow, and is it a few complaint types? | Which agency and complaint types to review first |
| 4. When and how | Hour-by-weekday heatmap; channel mix | When do requests arrive and how? | Staffing hours, and whether to push online channels |
| 5. Data quality | Rows before and after cleaning; each issue counted | Can these numbers be trusted? | Which data-entry problems to fix at the source |

Filters on every page: month, borough, agency. Each page ends with one written finding and one recommended action,
written only after the real results are in.
