Lead Follow-up Pipeline

A structured pipeline framework for organizing lead stages, ownership, priorities, follow-up dates and next actions so outstanding work can be tracked consistently.

Project scope: This page demonstrates the structure and reporting logic of a lead follow-up workflow. It does not present fictional records or fabricated performance metrics as business results.

Pipeline structure

Lead identification

Capture the lead identifier, company or account information, source and initial contact details.

Stage management

Track the current stage consistently from new lead through qualification, contact, proposal and closure.

Ownership

Assign an owner so every active lead has a clear person responsible for the next action.

Follow-up control

Record the next follow-up date, required action and priority to reduce missed or delayed follow-ups.

Recommended data fields

Field Purpose
Lead_ID Unique identifier for each lead or account.
Company Company or account associated with the opportunity.
Lead_Source Source through which the lead entered the pipeline.
Stage Current position in the follow-up or sales process.
Owner Person responsible for managing the lead.
Priority Relative urgency used to organize follow-up work.
Last_Activity_Date Date of the most recent recorded activity.
Next_Follow_Up_Date Planned date for the next action.
Next_Action Specific action required to move the lead forward.
Status Current operational status of the lead.

Pipeline stages

New

Lead has been added but has not yet received an initial follow-up.

Contacted

Initial outreach has been completed and the next interaction is being tracked.

Qualified

The lead has met the defined qualification criteria and requires continued follow-up.

Proposal

A proposal or commercial discussion is active and requires a clearly defined next action.

Won

The opportunity has reached a successful outcome.

Closed / Lost

The opportunity is no longer active and should include an appropriate closure reason where available.

Operational checks

  • Every active lead should have an assigned owner.
  • Active leads should have a current pipeline stage.
  • Follow-up dates should be reviewed for overdue actions.
  • Next actions should be specific enough to be actionable.
  • Closed leads should have an appropriate outcome or closure reason.
  • Duplicate Lead_ID values should be identified before reporting.
  • Missing ownership and follow-up dates should be flagged for review.

Useful pipeline reporting

Stage distribution

Monitor how records are distributed across pipeline stages and identify where work is concentrated.

Follow-ups due

Identify upcoming and overdue follow-ups so pending actions can be prioritized.

Owner workload

Compare active pipeline workload across owners and identify unassigned records.

Pipeline aging

Track how long active leads remain in each stage to identify potential bottlenecks.
